# ADR-0015 — Pixel-art device following the real device's silhouette

- Status: **Accepted** by the maintainer, who owns this decision and its risk
- Date: 2026-11-01
- Milestone: M5
- Changes: BRIEF §9, "Main screen layout"

## Context

BRIEF §9 asked for an original, clearly distinct shell, not copying Flipper Devices' industrial design.
The maintainer asked for a pixel-art device based on a picture of the real one, so that the site feels
like using the real thing, and for an organisation of the page modelled on how qFlipper is used. The
risk was explained: a look-alike of a commercial product's shape can raise trade-dress questions and
can make a site look official.

## Decision

- The device is drawn in pixel art following the real device's general silhouette: a landscape body with
  the screen on the left, a round D-pad on the right, an orange LCD. The drawing is generated by code
  (`web/src/ui/shell.ts`) on the same pixel grid as the LCD.
- **It stays dryflash:** the body says "dryflash". No "Flipper" wordmark, no mascot, no artwork or files
  from Flipper Devices or from the picture, whose rights are unknown. CLAUDE.md's branding rule and the
  "not affiliated" disclaimer stand.
- The page takes from qFlipper how it is used (device information, update, files, backup), not its
  graphics or files.

## Consequences

- Should Flipper Devices object, the shape alone must change: everything about it is in `shell.ts`.
- The disclaimer is on every page except the bare embed frame, where the host page is responsible.
